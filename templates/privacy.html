Privacy Policy

At Vyamir, your privacy is integral to our architectural design. We operate on a local-first privacy model to guarantee your atmospheric telemetry queries remain yours alone.

Data Collection and Usage

We do not store user location permanently. Precise coordinates are processed entirely in-memory to resolve environmental telemetry vectors and are never persisted beyond active session resolution.

Decentralized Synchronization

We prioritize user anonymity using our SkyID Vault logic. Any stored preferences or synchronization elements are associated with anonymous, decentralized nodes, ensuring no identity linkage occurs.

Third-Party Connections

Our platform requests high-fidelity meteorology records from Open-Meteo API clusters. Additionally, we integrate non-intrusive elements from the Google AdSense network to sustain global operations.

Contact Us

If you have any queries about our privacy protocol or data handling, please refer to our Contact Page.
