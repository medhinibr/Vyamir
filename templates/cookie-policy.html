Cookie Policy

Vyamir leverages modern client-side storage paradigms. Below is a detail of how local preference vectors are stored and used.

Strictly Necessary Preferences

We utilize client-side local storage keys (such as temperature unit preferences and anonymous authentication session states) to ensure the continuity of your environmental dashboard experience across reload actions.

Third-Party Tracking Cookies

Third-party networks, including Google AdSense, may place cookies on your device to display relevant, non-obtrusive advertisements. These cookies can be blocked or cleared directly via your browser preferences without degrading Vyamir's core telemetry engine.

Managing Storage Preferences

You can adjust, clear, or restrict cookies and local storage tokens at any time through your browser's security/privacy configuration tab.
